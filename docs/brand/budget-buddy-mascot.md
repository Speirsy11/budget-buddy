# Budget Buddy Mascot Assets

Budget Buddy's mascot is a friendly, mint-green budgeting companion designed to make personal finance feel calm, approachable, and non-judgemental.

## Creative direction

- Helpful companion, not a bank or accountant.
- Calm, friendly, practical, and trustworthy.
- Soft mint/teal palette with rounded shapes.
- Finance cues: coins, receipt, checklist, wallet, lock, charts, and savings icons.
- Avoid luxury/wealth imagery, suits, corporate banking clichés, or intimidating finance visuals.
